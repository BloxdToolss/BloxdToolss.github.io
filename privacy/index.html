Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

1. Data Collection via Discord OAuth

When you log into BloxdTools using your Discord account, our third-party data secure wrapper (Supabase Auth) requests minimal public identification scopes. We collect your public Discord username, unique identifier, profile picture, account banner, and verified email address.

2. How Your Information is Used

Your data is processed strictly to provide a customized user experience. Your email address and unique identity are used as database primary keys to securely save your uploaded HTML layouts, moderator submission rankings, and personal hub configuration files. We do not sell, rent, leak, or distribute user email records or authorization profiles to any third-party marketing entities.

3. Local Data Storage & Cookies

To avoid unneeded database calls and maximize site performance, your active layout preferences (such as toggling between the BloxdHub 4.0 dark theme and the Bloxd.io interface look) are saved client-side utilizing your browser's native localStorage sandbox engine. No tracking scripts or commercial data cookies are deployed.

4. Data Deletion and Account Revocation

Users maintain complete ownership over their profiles. If you wish to wipe your records from our databases, you can do so instantly by heading to your settings panel or revoking app access directly through your native Discord client settings via User Settings > Authorized Apps.
